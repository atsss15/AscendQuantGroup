Lake Lurkers

A real-life game of chores, secrets and shoulder taps. The Campers keep the lake house running. Hidden among them: 2 Lurkers. Find them before they get you.

Times on this page are the defaults. The host can change them, and the game app always shows the live numbers.

The 60-second version

  1. Sign in at ascend-exchange.fly.dev/lake with your name and a 4-digit PIN. Meet at the Campfire: the Great Room, by the big fireplace.
  2. Peek at your role (press and hold the role card). Most of you are Campers. 2 Lurkers hide among you, and they know each other.
  3. Campers scan the QR codes around the house and do their chores. Fill the chore bar or vote out every Lurker to win.
  4. Lurkers blend in, fake chores, and kill Campers: a tap on the shoulder, then confirm on the phone.
  5. Tapped? Sit or lie down right there, stay silent, and show your 3-digit body code.
  6. Found a body? Type its code under Report a body and shout “Campfire!” Everyone walks to the Great Room.
  7. At the Campfire, talk for 3 min, then vote on your phone. Most votes (and more than Skip) is out. A tie means nobody.
  8. Out? You’re a ghost: wear a ghost marker, stay silent, keep doing chores.
  9. Storm! When the Lurkers start one, two different players must secure Upstairs Hall and Screened Patio within 90 sec, or the Lurkers win.
  10. Talk any time. Walk, don’t run. Stay out of the water. Shout “PAUSE” for anything real.

The full rules

The goal and the roles

Lurkers: 2 (1 if 6 or fewer play) Chores each: 5

Campers

  • Do your chores around the house.
  • Watch everyone. Remember who was where.
  • Vote out the Lurkers at Campfires.

Lurkers

  • Act like a Camper. Fake your chores.
  • Kill Campers one by one and start storms.
  • You see the other Lurker’s name on your role card. Work together.

Never show your role card to anyone. Say whatever you like, but your screen is never proof. Everyone’s app looks the same, so a glance at someone’s phone gives nothing away.

Getting started

  1. Open ascend-exchange.fly.dev/lake in your phone’s browser, or scan the Join QR on the TV. Nothing to install.
  2. Sign in with your name and a 4-digit PIN you’ll remember. The same name and PIN get you back in on any device.
  3. Turn your volume up and leave Keep screen on switched on. Your phone beeps for Campfires, storms and photo requests, but only while the game page is open.
  4. Meet at the Campfire: the Great Room, by the big fireplace, where the TV is. The TV shows the chore bar, who’s out, Campfire timers and storms.
  5. The host taps Start. Peek at your role, check your chore list, and spread out. No kills for the first 60 sec.

Want to skip a game? Tap Sit out in the lobby before it starts.

Chores

You get 3 tasks, 1 photo and 1 carry, at stations all over the house. Your chore list says where to go. Scan the station, then:

  • Task: a quick puzzle on your phone (10 to 30 sec). Hold a button down (let go and it restarts), tap 1 to 10 in order, repeat a color pattern, or match the pairs.
  • Photo: take a photo at the station. Some need another living player in the shot. Pick them in the app, and they tap Yes on their own phone to confirm. Every photo goes into the gallery at the end, so make it a good one.
  • Carry: scan the pick-up station to grab the item (it’s imaginary), then walk it over and scan the drop-off station.

The chore bar

The bar on every phone and on the TV fills as Campers finish chores. When it’s full, the Campers win. Lurkers get chore lists too, but theirs never count, and the app never says whose chores counted.

This game the bar only moves at Campfires, so you can’t watch it to catch a fake chore.

No chores during a Campfire or while the game is paused.

Talking

Talk any time, anywhere, not just at Campfires. Share what you saw, accuse, team up, bluff. Lurkers are allowed to lie.

Two exceptions: bodies and ghosts stay completely silent. And keep it face to face: no texting, calls or group chats about the game while it’s running.

Kills

For Lurkers

  1. Give a Camper a light tap on the shoulder.
  2. Confirm on your phone right away: open your role card, tap Kill, pick their name.
  • Wait 2½ min between kills. The Kill button counts down.
  • No kills in the first 60 sec of a game, or for 90 sec after a Campfire.
  • No kills during a Campfire or a pause. Lurkers can’t kill each other, and ghosts can’t kill.
  • Never tap anyone on the stairs or near the water.

If you’re killed

  • Your phone shows who got you and a big 3-digit body code, like 417.
  • Sit or lie down right where you are. On the stairs or the dock? Step to a safe spot first.
  • Stay silent. No talking, no faces, no pointing. Hold your phone so whoever finds you can read the code.
  • Stay put until a Campfire starts. Then you get up as a ghost.

Nobody tapped you? If your phone says you’re dead but nobody touched you, press I wasn’t tapped within 20 sec (only before a Campfire starts). You’re alive again. Honor system: only if it’s true.

And the other way round: you’re only dead when your phone says so. A tap with no confirm means nothing.

Bodies and the bell

Found a body?

  1. Read the 3-digit code on their phone.
  2. Type it under Report a body on your phone.
  3. Shout “Campfire!” A locked phone can’t buzz, so your voice is the alarm.

Anyone alive can report a body, Lurkers included. After 5 wrong codes the box locks for 30 sec.

Ring the bell

Want a Campfire without a body? Go to the Campfire, scan its QR, tap Ring the bell, and shout “Campfire!”

  • Each player can ring it once per game.
  • Not during a storm, and not in the first 30 sec after a Campfire.
  • Ghosts can’t ring it.

Campfire

A report or the bell starts a Campfire for everyone: phones sound an alarm and so does the TV. Stop what you’re doing (chores freeze anyway) and walk to the Great Room.

  1. 1. Gather 60 sec
    Come to the Great Room and tap I’m here. Moves on early once everyone’s in.
  2. 2. Discuss 3 min
    Phones and the TV show who called it, whose body, and everyone who died since the last Campfire. Talk it out.
  3. 3. Vote 60 sec
    Vote on your phone for a living player, or Skip. One vote, no take-backs. Ends early once everyone has voted.
  4. 4. Result 12 sec
    The votes appear on every phone and the TV. Then the game goes on.

Who goes out

The player with the most votes is out, but only if nobody ties them and they got more votes than Skip. Otherwise nobody goes out. Players who don’t vote don’t count.

  • Ana 4, Ben 2, Skip 3: Ana is out.
  • Ana 3, Ben 3, Skip 1: tie, nobody.
  • Ana 3, Skip 3: not more than Skip, nobody.
  • Votes are public: everyone sees who voted for whom.
  • Votes are secret this game: you only see the totals.
  • Roles stay hidden: nobody learns whether the player voted out was a Camper or a Lurker.
  • Roles are shown this game: everyone learns whether the player voted out was a Camper or a Lurker.
  • Every body is revealed at a Campfire, found or not.
  • Afterwards: no kills for 90 sec, no bell for 30 sec, no storm for 60 sec.

Ghosts

You become a ghost when a Campfire reveals your body or when you’re voted out.

  • Put on a ghost marker (a bandana or glow stick from the host) so everyone can tell.
  • Stay silent. No talking, texting, pointing or faces. You know things. Keep them to yourself.
  • Keep doing chores. A Camper ghost’s chores still fill the bar. Your photo chores no longer need a partner.
  • Ghosts can’t vote, report bodies, ring the bell, secure a storm, or be in someone’s photo.
  • You can watch Campfires. Quietly.
  • A Lurker ghost can still start storms, but can’t kill.

The storm

Storms are switched off this game.

The Lurkers can start a storm from their phones. Every phone plays a siren and shows a countdown, and the TV turns red. You have 90 sec to secure both storm stations:

  • Upstairs HallUpstairs, the hallway outside the primary bedroom
  • Screened PatioOutside the basement living room, lake side
  • To secure one, scan its QR and hold Secure for the storm for 3 sec.
  • It takes two different living players, one at each station. Ghosts can’t help.
  • Both secured: the storm is over. Time runs out: the Lurkers win.
  • Reporting a body cancels the storm, because the Campfire starts. You can’t ring the bell during a storm.
  • The first storm can come 3 min into a game. After that, at least 4 min after the last storm ends, and 60 sec after a Campfire.

Shout who’s going where. Walk fast, but don’t run.

How each side wins

Campers win when

  • every Lurker has been voted out, or
  • the chore bar is full.

Lurkers win when

  • living Lurkers equal or outnumber living Campers (say, 2 Lurkers and 2 Campers left), or
  • a storm runs out of time.

The end screen reveals every role, who killed whom and when, and the game’s photos.

Safety

  • Walk, don’t run. Inside, outside, on the stairs, and especially during a storm.
  • Shoulder taps only. A light tap. Never push, grab, tackle or jump-scare anyone. No taps on the stairs or near the water.
  • Stay out of the water. On the dock, keep away from the edges and never get on a boat.
  • Phone in your hand, not your pocket, near the water. Pockets are how phones end up in the lake.
  • No unsafe hiding: no closets, cabinets, under furniture, on roofs, or anywhere you could get stuck or hurt. Don’t lock or block doors.
  • Out of bounds stays out: bedrooms, bathrooms, the water, the road, cars, garages.

Shout “PAUSE” for anything real: someone gets hurt, something spills or breaks, a phone goes in the water, someone’s at the door. Everyone freezes, the host pauses the app (all timers and cooldowns stop), and we pick up again when it’s sorted.

Tips

Campers

  • Move in pairs or small groups. It’s harder to get tapped with witnesses around.
  • Remember who you saw where, and when. Campfires are won with alibis.
  • Watching someone do a chore proves nothing: Lurkers have chore lists too. Same for photos. Being in someone’s photo doesn’t clear them.
  • Save your bell for a real lead. You only get once.
  • Storm? Decide in seconds who goes up and who goes down, and say it out loud.
  • Not sure? Vote Skip. Voting out a Camper helps the Lurkers.
  • Ghost? Keep doing chores. The bar still needs you.

Lurkers

  • Check that your Kill button is ready before you tap. A tap you can’t confirm gives you away.
  • Kill where nobody’s looking, then walk off calmly. Or “find” the body and report it yourself.
  • Do your fake chores. Standing at a station is an alibi.
  • A storm sends Campers to opposite ends of the house, alone. You can still kill during a storm, but a reported body ends it.
  • Talk. Accuse, defend, vouch. For your partner, or against them if it saves you.
  • Don’t stick to your partner all game. Two people who always vouch for each other look guilty.
  • Voted out? Ghost Lurkers can still start storms.

FAQ

I lost my phone, or got signed out.

Sign in again with the same name and PIN, on any phone. You’re right back where you were. If you borrowed a phone, sign out afterwards.

The QR code won’t scan.

Type the code printed under it into the Scan box in the game.

I’m dead and nobody’s finding me.

Stay put and stay silent. The next Campfire reveals every body, found or not. Then you get up as a ghost.

Someone tapped me, but my phone didn’t change.

You’re alive until your phone says you’re dead. As for whoever tapped you: now you know something.

My phone says I’m dead, but nobody tapped me.

Press “I wasn’t tapped” within 20 sec. It only works before a Campfire starts.

Can I show my phone to prove I’m a Camper?

No. Never show your role card. Talk your way out.

Do I have to come to every Campfire?

Yes, if you’re alive. Drop what you’re doing and walk over. Chores are frozen anyway.

What if I don’t vote?

Your vote just doesn’t count. Skip is a real option if you’re unsure.

I typed a body code wrong.

Try again. After 5 wrong codes the box locks for 30 sec.

My phone didn’t make a sound.

Phones only beep while the game page is open, and a locked iPhone never buzzes. Keep the page open with Keep screen on, turn the volume up, and listen for “Campfire!” and the TV.

Bathroom or snack break?

Out-of-bounds rooms are safe: no kills, no chores. Keep it short. For anything longer, ask the host to pause.

Can Lurkers kill each other, or kill a ghost?

No and no.

I want to sit a game out.

Tap Sit out in the lobby before the game starts.

Who’s the host?

Whoever signed in as host. They can play too. The host can start, pause and end games, skip ahead in a Campfire, and change settings like timers. The game app always shows the current numbers.
