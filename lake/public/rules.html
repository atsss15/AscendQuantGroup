Lake Lurkers

A real-life game of chores, secrets and shoulder taps. The Campers keep the lake house running. Hidden among them: 2 Lurkers. Find them before they get you.

Times on this page are the defaults. The host can change them between games, and the game app always shows the live numbers.

The 60-second version

  1. Sign in at ascend-exchange.fly.dev/lake with your name and a 4-digit PIN. Meet at the Campfire: the Great Room, by the big fireplace.
  2. Peek at your role: press and hold the role card, then tap Hide. Most of you are Campers. Hidden among you: 2 Lurkers, and Lurkers know each other.
  3. Your chores are secret, even from you. My rooms and the 🗺 Map only show which rooms to go to. In each room, scan its QR code: tap 📷 Scan QR in the game (or use your phone’s camera, or type the code under it). Then you see what to do there.
  4. Do them: 2 phone games, 2 activities, 1 photo and 1 carry. Activities are for real: hold a plank, sing on the radio, dance by the fire. Tap ▶ Start, keep going until the timer runs out, then tap Done.
  5. Phone games (Snake, Wordle, Blackjack and more) take about 2 min. Lose one, or quit it, and there’s no second try: the game moves to another room, usually on another floor. Go find it.
  6. Campers win by filling the chore bar or voting out every Lurker.
  7. Lurkers blend in, fake chores, and kill Campers: a tap on the shoulder, then confirm the kill on their phone.
  8. Killed? Your phone tells you. Sit or lie down right there, stay silent, and show your 3-digit body code.
  9. Found a body? Type its code in the Found a body? box, tap Report, and shout “Campfire!” Everyone walks to the Great Room.
  10. At the Campfire, tap I’m here, talk for 3 min, then vote: tap a name or Skip, then Yes, vote. Most votes (and more than Skip) is out. A tie means nobody.
  11. Out? You’re a ghost: wear a ghost marker, stay silent, keep doing chores.
  12. Storm! When the Lurkers start one, two different living players must secure Upstairs Hall and Screened Patio within 90 sec, or the Lurkers win.
  13. Talk any time. Walk, don’t run. Stay out of the water. Shout “PAUSE” for anything real.

The full rules

The goal and the roles

Lurkers: 2 (1 if 6 or fewer play) Chores each: 6

Campers

  • Do your chores around the house.
  • Watch everyone. Remember who was where.
  • Vote out the Lurkers at Campfires.

Lurkers

  • Act like a Camper. You get a chore list too: do it anyway, it just never counts.
  • Kill Campers one by one and start storms.
  • Your role card tells you who the other Lurker is (if there is one). Work together.

Your role card

Press and hold the card on the game page to see your role. It stays open (Lurkers kill from it) until you tap Hide, and it closes by itself after 15 sec without a tap on it, or when you lock your phone.

Never show your role card to anyone. Say whatever you like, but your screen is never proof. Everyone’s app looks the same until the card is held, so a glance at someone’s phone gives nothing away. Open it where nobody can see your screen.

Getting started

  1. Open ascend-exchange.fly.dev/lake in your phone’s browser, or scan the Join QR on the TV. Nothing to install.
  2. Sign in with your name and a 4-digit PIN you’ll remember. The same name and PIN get you back in on any device.
  3. Make sure you’ll hear the alarms. Volume up (on newer iPhones the game plays even on silent; on older ones, turn the ringer on too), and the button at the bottom of the game page should say 🔊 Sound on.
  4. Keep the game page open with the screen on. During a game the bottom of the page shows ☀️ Screen stays on: leave it like that. No such button? Set Auto-Lock to Never while you play. Your phone beeps for Campfires, storms, and photo and talk requests only while the game page is open and the screen is on, and iPhones never vibrate for it.
  5. Meet at the Campfire: the Great Room, by the big fireplace, where the TV is. The TV shows the chore bar, who’s out, Campfire timers and storms.
  6. The host starts the game. Peek at your role, check which rooms your chores are in (My rooms or the 🗺 Map), and spread out. No kills for the first 60 sec, and no bell for the first 30 sec.
  7. The first time you tap 📷 Scan QR, your browser asks to use the camera: tap Allow.

Want to skip a game? Switch on Sit out the next game in the lobby before the host starts.

Scanning and the map

Scanning a station’s QR code opens it in the game: that’s where you see and do your chores, secure a storm, and, at the Campfire, ring the bell. Any of these works:

  1. Right in the game. Tap 📷 Scan QR on the game page and point your phone at the card. It scans in the web page itself: no app, no switching. The first time, tap Allow when your browser asks for the camera.
  2. Your phone’s camera app. Point it at the card and tap the link that pops up. If that opens a different browser from the one you play in, sign in there with the same name and PIN, or use 📷 Scan QR instead.
  3. Type the code. Enter the 4 characters printed under the QR, like K7QM, in the At a station? box and tap Go.

The 🗺 Map

Tap 🗺 Map in the game (or a room in My rooms) for floor plans of the main floor, upstairs and the basement. Every station has a marker:

  • a number: that many of your chores are there, still to do;
  • a ✓: your chores there are done;
  • a plain dot: nothing for you there. 🔥 is the Campfire.

Tap a marker to see its name and where it is. The Dock, the Lakeshore and the Radio aren’t on the floor plans: they’re under the Outside tab. The map only shows your own chores, never anyone else’s.

Chores

You get 2 phone games, 2 activities, 1 photo and 1 carry, all over the house. Lurkers get a list too.

Secret until you get there

At the start, My rooms only tells you which rooms you have chores in, not what they are. The 🗺 Map marks the same rooms. Go to one and scan its QR code (any of the three ways): your chores there appear, and you can do them right away. One room can hold more than one of your chores. As you go, My rooms and the map show which rooms you’ve finished (✓).

  • A carry shows up at its pick-up room. Once you’ve picked it up, the list says where to take it.
  • The talk chore is never secret: it’s on your list from the start, and you do it anywhere.
  • A phone game you lose moves to a new room and is secret again until you scan that room.
  • Scanned a room with nothing for you? The game says so. Nobody else can see your chores, or which rooms you’ve scanned.

Phone games

Seven games, each about 2 minutes, played on your own phone in the room. Scan the room, tap the game, and win it: that’s the chore done.

Lose it and you can’t start over. The game moves to another room, usually on another floor, and turns secret again. My rooms and the 🗺 Map show the new room: go there, scan it, and play from the start.

Quitting counts as losing. Tap Quit in a game you’ve started and the app asks: “Quit? It counts as a loss and this game moves to another room.” Reloading the page in the middle of a game counts as quitting too.

A Campfire, a storm or a pause that closes your game is not a loss: the game stays put, so start it again in the same room.

Where each game starts (one of two rooms), how to win it, and how you lose it. A moved game can turn up anywhere but the Campfire, outside included.

  • Hold the line2 minLose: let goUpstairs Hall · Screened Patio: hold the button down for 2 minutes. Let go for more than a second and you lose.
  • Wordle6 triesLose: 6 missesUpstairs Landing · Basement Office: guess the 5-letter word in 6 tries. Six wrong guesses and you lose.
  • Make 243 handsCan’t loseDining Room · Basement Kitchen: make 24 from four cards with + − × ÷. Solve 3 hands. Stuck? Skip hand deals a new hand (skipped hands don’t count).
  • Snake12 applesLose: crashSunroom · Media Room: eat 12 apples. Hit a wall or yourself and you lose.
  • Blackjack3 winsLose: 3 lossesEast Living Room · Basement Living Room: beat the dealer 3 times before the dealer beats you 3 times.
  • Tap 1 to 503 sec freezeCan’t loseKitchen · Laundry Room: tap the numbers 1 to 50 in order. A wrong tap freezes you for 3 sec.
  • Crack the code2 digitsCan’t loseOffice · Foyer: brute-force the 2-digit code (the Wi-Fi password, or the lockbox), one try at a time. It just takes tries.

Eyes up. Two minutes staring at your phone is two minutes you’re not watching your back.

Activities

  1. Go to the station and scan its QR code.
  2. Tap the activity, read what to do, then tap ▶ Start. A timer counts down.
  3. Do the activity, for real, while it runs.
  4. When the time is up, tap Done. Tapping early doesn’t work.

Honor system: do the whole activity before you tap Done. The timer only stops you from finishing early. It can’t see you, but everyone else can.

Some take a few tries: ping pong, table curling and the golf sim (all three in the basement), rock, paper, scissors and skipping a stone. There the timer is only the minimum. Keep going until you’ve really done it, then tap Done (at the ping pong table, the curling board and the golf sim it says I did it!).

Unlike phone games, activities can’t be lost and never move. Every activity, by where it is. Out loud means everyone around will hear you.

The talk chore

One of your activities can be Catch up with 3 people, which you do anywhere, with no scan. Tap 💬 Talk to someone on that chore in My rooms (under 💬 Anywhere) and pick a living player. Your phone gives you a question for them, like “What’s your go-to karaoke song?” Find them and ask it, face to face.

  • Their phone asks them: “Did your name just ask you: …?” When they tap Yes, that’s one. Three different people saying yes finishes the chore, and the chore shows how many you have.
  • One ask at a time: picking someone new replaces the one still waiting. Someone who already said yes doesn’t count twice.
  • They tapped No, or never saw it? Ask again, or pick someone else.

When someone asks you to confirm

Took a photo with someone, or got asked their question? Your phone pops up a check: “Were you in this photo with …?” or “Did … just ask you: …?” Tap Yes only if it really happened, No if it didn’t. Anyone alive can be asked, Lurkers included. Ghosts can’t.

The chore bar

The bar on every phone and on the TV fills as Campers finish chores. When it’s full, the Campers win. Lurkers’ chores never count, and the app never says whose chores counted.

This game the bar only moves at Campfires, so you can’t watch it to catch a fake chore.

No chores during a Campfire or while the game is paused. A Campfire, a storm or a pause can close a game or activity you’re in the middle of (that never counts as a loss): start it again afterwards, in the same room.

Talking

Talk any time, anywhere, not just at Campfires. Share what you saw, accuse, team up, bluff. Lurkers are allowed to lie.

Two exceptions: bodies and ghosts stay completely silent. And keep it face to face: no texting, calls or group chats about the game while it’s running.

Kills

For Lurkers

  1. Give a Camper a light tap on the shoulder.
  2. Confirm on your phone right away: press and hold your role card, tap their name under Kill, then tap Yes, kill …
  • Wait 2½ min between kills. Until your next kill is ready, the names are greyed out and the card shows a countdown, like Ready in 1:30.
  • No kills in the first 60 sec of a game. Every Campfire resets the Lurkers’ kills: none for 90 sec after it ends.
  • No kills during a Campfire or a pause. Only living Campers are on the list: Lurkers can’t kill each other, nobody can kill a ghost, and ghosts can’t kill.
  • Never tap anyone on the stairs, near the water, or while they’re swinging a club or paddle.

If you’re killed

  • Your phone says You’ve been got!, shows who got you, and a big 3-digit body code, like 417.
  • Sit or lie down right where you are. On the stairs or the dock? Step to a safe spot first.
  • Stay silent. No talking, no faces, no pointing. Keep the screen on and hold your phone so whoever finds you can read the code.
  • Stay put until a Campfire starts. Then you get up as a ghost.

Nobody tapped you? If your phone says you’re dead but nobody touched you, press I wasn’t tapped! within 20 sec. The button disappears when the time is up, and it stops working once a Campfire starts. You’re alive again, and the Lurker gets their kill back. Honor system: only if it’s true.

And the other way round: you’re only dead when your phone says so. A tap with no confirm means nothing.

Bodies and the bell

Found a body?

  1. Read the 3-digit code on their phone.
  2. Type it in the Found a body? box on your phone and tap Report.
  3. Shout “Campfire!” A locked phone can’t beep, so your voice is the alarm.

Anyone alive can report a body, Lurkers included. After 5 wrong codes the box locks for 30 sec.

Ring the bell

Want a Campfire without a body? Go to the Campfire, scan its QR, tap Ring the bell, then Ring it! to confirm, and shout “Campfire!”

  • Each player can ring it once per game.
  • Not in the first 30 sec of a game or after a Campfire (the button counts down), and not during a storm.
  • Ghosts can’t ring it.

Campfire

A report or the bell starts a Campfire for everyone: phones flash and sound an alarm, and so does the TV. Stop what you’re doing (chores freeze anyway) and walk to the Great Room.

  1. 1. Gather 60 sec
    Come to the Great Room and tap I’m here. Moves on early once every living player is in.
  2. 2. Discuss 3 min
    Phones and the TV show who called it, whose body, and everyone killed since the last Campfire. Talk it out.
  3. 3. Vote 60 sec
    Tap a living player or Skip, then Yes, vote to confirm. Back lets you pick again, but once you’ve confirmed, your vote is final. Ends early once everyone has voted.
  4. 4. Result 12 sec
    The votes appear on every phone and the TV. Then the game goes on.

Who goes out

The player with the most votes is out, but only if nobody ties them and they got more votes than Skip. Otherwise nobody goes out. Players who don’t vote don’t count.

  • Ana 4, Ben 2, Skip 3: Ana is out.
  • Ana 3, Ben 3, Skip 1: tie, nobody.
  • Ana 3, Skip 3: not more than Skip, nobody.
  • Votes are public: everyone sees who voted for whom.
  • Votes are secret this game: you only see the totals.
  • Roles stay hidden: nobody learns whether the player voted out was a Camper or a Lurker.
  • Roles are shown this game: everyone learns whether the player voted out was a Camper or a Lurker.
  • Every body is revealed at a Campfire, found or not.
  • Afterwards: no kills for 90 sec, no bell for 30 sec, no storm for 60 sec.

Ghosts

You become a ghost when a Campfire reveals your body or when you’re voted out. Your phone says 👻 You’re a ghost.

  • Put on a ghost marker (a bandana or glow stick from the host) so everyone can tell.
  • Stay silent. No talking, texting, pointing or faces. You know things. Keep them to yourself.
  • Keep doing chores. A Camper ghost’s chores still fill the bar. You still scan rooms to see what’s there.
  • Phone games, photosPhotos and carries work as usual, just silently (lose a phone game and it still moves). Your photo chores no longer need a partner.
  • Silent activities: anything Out loud (the radio song and shout-out, the impression, the movie scene) you do without a sound: lip-sync or mime it until the timer runs out. Every other activity works as usual, quietly.
  • Silent talk chore: Catch up with 3 people becomes a 30 sec timer (👋 Wave at players): silently wave at 3 different players while it runs. Nobody has to confirm.
  • Ghosts can’t vote, report bodies, ring the bell, secure a storm, be in someone’s photo, or answer someone’s talk question.
  • You can watch Campfires. Quietly.
  • A Lurker ghost can still start storms, but can’t kill.

The storm

Storms are switched off this game.

The Lurkers start storms from their role card: ⛈ Start a storm, then Start it. Every phone plays a siren and shows a countdown, and the TV turns red. You have 90 sec to secure both storm stations:

  • Upstairs HallUpstairs, the hallway outside the primary bedroom
  • Screened PatioOutside the basement living room, lake side
  • To secure one, scan its QR, then press and hold Hold to secure (3 s) until it fills. Let go early and it starts over.
  • It takes two different living players, one at each station. Ghosts can’t help.
  • Both secured: the storm is over. Time runs out: the Lurkers win.
  • Reporting a body cancels the storm, because the Campfire starts. You can’t ring the bell during a storm.
  • The first storm can come 3 min into a game. After that, at least 4 min after the last storm ends, and 60 sec after a Campfire.

Shout who’s going where. Walk fast, but don’t run.

How each side wins

Campers win when

  • every Lurker has been voted out, or
  • the chore bar is full.

Lurkers win when

  • living Lurkers equal or outnumber living Campers (say, 2 Lurkers and 2 Campers left), or
  • a storm runs out of time.

The end screen reveals every role, who killed whom and when, and the game’s photos.

Safety

  • Walk, don’t run. Inside, outside, on the stairs, and especially during a storm.
  • Eyes up when you move. Play phone games standing or sitting still, never while walking or on the stairs.
  • Shoulder taps only. A light tap. Never push, grab, tackle or jump-scare anyone. No taps on the stairs, near the water, or mid-swing.
  • Golf sim and ping pong: look around before you swing, and give whoever’s playing room.
  • Activities at your own pace. Push-ups on your knees are fine, and nobody has to do anything that hurts. Skip stones from the shore only, away from people.
  • Stay out of the water. On the dock, keep away from the edges and never get on a boat.
  • Phone in your hand, not your pocket, near the water. Pockets are how phones end up in the lake.
  • No unsafe hiding: no closets, cabinets, under furniture, on roofs, or anywhere you could get stuck or hurt. Don’t lock or block doors.
  • Out of bounds stays out: bedrooms, bathrooms, the water, the road, cars, garages.

Shout “PAUSE” for anything real: someone gets hurt, something spills or breaks, a phone goes in the water, someone’s at the door. Everyone freezes, the host pauses the app (all timers and cooldowns stop, and nobody can do anything until it’s back on), and we pick up again when it’s sorted.

Tips

Campers

  • Move in pairs or small groups. It’s harder to get tapped with witnesses around.
  • Remember who you saw where, and when. Campfires are won with alibis.
  • Watching someone do a chore proves nothing: Lurkers have chore lists too. Same for photos and talk questions. Being in someone’s photo, or answering their question, doesn’t clear them.
  • Nobody can see anyone’s chores, not even their own before they scan. “I had a chore in the kitchen” is a claim, not proof.
  • Playing a phone game? Do it near someone, and look up now and then.
  • Lost a phone game? Its new room is often on another floor. Don’t make that walk alone if you can help it.
  • Hear someone on the radio? Right then, you know exactly where they are.
  • Save your bell for a real lead. You can only ring it once.
  • Storm? Decide in seconds who goes up and who goes down, and say it out loud.
  • Not sure? Vote Skip. Voting out a Camper helps the Lurkers.
  • Ghost? Keep doing chores. The bar still needs you.

Lurkers

  • Check that your kill is ready (names not greyed out) before you tap. A tap you can’t confirm gives you away.
  • Kill where nobody’s looking, then walk off calmly. Or “find” the body and report it yourself.
  • Do your chores, for real. They don’t count, but a plank in the hallway is a great alibi. Scan rooms the way a Camper would.
  • A Camper alone, head down in a 2-minute phone game, isn’t watching the door. One who just lost a game is about to cross the house, maybe alone.
  • Got the talk chore? It’s the perfect excuse to walk up to anyone.
  • A storm sends Campers to opposite ends of the house, alone. You can still kill during a storm, but a reported body ends it.
  • Talk. Accuse, defend, vouch. For your partner, or against them if it saves you.
  • Don’t stick to your partner all game. Two people who always vouch for each other look guilty.
  • Voted out? Ghost Lurkers can still start storms.

FAQ

I lost my phone, or got signed out.

Sign in again with the same name and PIN, on any phone. You’re right back where you were. If you borrowed a phone, tap Sign out at the bottom of the page afterwards.

Why doesn’t my chore list say what my chores are?

That’s the point: you find out in the room. My rooms and the 🗺 Map show which rooms to go to. Scan a room’s QR code and your chores there appear.

The QR code won’t scan.

Try the other way: tap “📷 Scan QR” on the game page, or use your phone’s camera app. Still nothing? Type the 4-character code printed under the QR into the “At a station?” box on the game page and tap Go.

“📷 Scan QR” doesn’t show the camera.

The game page needs your permission to use the camera: tap Allow when the browser asks. Said no by mistake? Reload the page and try again, or allow the camera for this site in your browser’s settings. Typing the code always works.

I’m dead and nobody’s finding me.

Stay put and stay silent. The next Campfire reveals every body, found or not. Then you get up as a ghost.

Someone tapped me, but my phone didn’t change.

You’re alive until your phone says you’re dead. As for whoever tapped you: now you know something.

My phone says I’m dead, but nobody tapped me.

Press “I wasn’t tapped!” within 20 sec. It only works before a Campfire starts.

I lost a phone game. Can I try again?

Not in that room. A lost game moves to another room, usually on another floor, and turns secret again. My rooms and the 🗺 Map show where it went: go there, scan the room, and play it from the start. You can lose it there too, and it moves again.

Can I quit a phone game halfway?

You can, but it counts as losing: tapping Quit asks “Quit? It counts as a loss and this game moves to another room.” Tap Cancel there and you’re back in the game. Reloading the page mid-game counts as quitting too.

A Campfire started in the middle of my phone game.

The game closes, and so does any activity you were doing. That’s not a loss: nothing moves. After the Campfire, go back to the same room and start it again. Same if a storm or a pause closes it.

My photo partner never answered.

Maybe their phone is asleep, or they tapped “Not now” (they can still answer later from their game page). Whatever the reason, go back to the station and tap “Retake with someone else”. The chore only counts once your partner taps Yes.

Do I really have to sing?

Yes, that’s the chore, and the whole house is listening. Everything is on the honor system, but the timer won’t let you skip ahead. Ghosts lip-sync instead.

Can I tap Done without doing the activity?

The app can’t stop you, but that’s cheating. Honor system: do the whole thing, then tap Done. Can’t do one (a sore back, say)? Do the gentlest version you can, like push-ups on your knees.

Someone’s already at the radio, the table or the sim.

Wait your turn, or go do another chore and come back. Only a few players get each activity per game.

Nobody’s saying yes to my talk question.

Their phone may be asleep: ask them to open the game page. Or pick someone else: a new ask replaces the one still waiting. You need 3 different people.

Can I show my phone to prove I’m a Camper?

No. Never show your role card. Talk your way out.

Do I have to come to every Campfire?

Yes, if you’re alive. Drop what you’re doing and walk over. Chores are frozen anyway.

I tapped the wrong name when voting.

Nothing happens until you tap “Yes, vote”. Tap “Back” and pick again. Once you’ve tapped “Yes, vote”, it’s final.

What if I don’t vote?

Your vote just doesn’t count. Skip is a real option if you’re unsure.

I typed a body code wrong.

Try again. After 5 wrong codes the box locks for 30 sec.

My phone didn’t make a sound.

Phones only beep while the game page is open and the screen is on. Check that the volume is up (older iPhones also need the ringer on), and the button at the bottom says “Sound on”. After your phone has been asleep, tap the screen once to wake the sound up. iPhones never vibrate for the game, so listen for “Campfire!” and the TV.

The page says “Reconnecting…”.

The Wi-Fi dropped. Wait a few seconds, or reload the page (but not in the middle of a phone game: that counts as quitting it. Just keep playing; a win saves once you’re back online). Nothing is lost: the game keeps going and you’re signed right back in. If the whole server restarted, the game comes back paused and the host presses Resume.

Bathroom or snack break?

Out-of-bounds rooms are safe: no kills, no chores. Keep it short. For anything longer, ask the host to pause.

Can Lurkers kill each other, or kill a ghost?

No and no.

I want to sit a game out.

Switch on “Sit out the next game” in the lobby before the host starts. The host can also sit you out.

Who’s the host?

Whoever signed in as host: open ascend-exchange.fly.dev/lake/admin, enter the admin password, and tap “▶ Start the game” in the lobby. The host can play too, on the same phone. The host can start, pause and end games, move a Campfire to its next stage early, sit players out or remove them, and change the settings (timers, chores, storms) between games, never during one. The game app always shows the current numbers.
